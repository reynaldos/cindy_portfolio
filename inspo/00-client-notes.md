# Cindy's own notes (from inspo/design-2.png)

These are real requests written on the mockup. Treat them as requirements. The art style of the mockup (painted Ghibli scenes, Totoro and soot-sprite doodles) is NOT a requirement.

## Requests
1. Page transition: "Similar to the photography website, I'd like this to have an effect of the leaves opening up to what ever page they click at the top." A reveal transition when a nav link is clicked: a layer parts or opens to show the next page. Leaves are her example; the idea is an "opening" reveal.
2. Projects: four categories: Short Films, Commercials, Music Videos, Trailers. Clicking one opens a page in the same style with all videos in that category.
3. Category page: "similar to the chat gpt photo design where we can scroll through the vids / see a description about them." A scrollable video list, each with a description.
4. Recent Cuts: a featured section with copy and a grid of 5 video slots.
5. About: photo, bio, tools, location.

## Real content
- Brand: Cuts By Cindy. Tagline: "Video Editor. Sharing stories of culture and connection."
- Recent Cuts copy: Commercial work with Mainstay Films, a Rod Wave Production Company. Five commercial spots for Wave Wellness, a longevity and wellness company in North Carolina. Rough cut to final delivery in 10 days. Several feedback rounds; final versions for broadcast and social.
- About: "Hi I'm Cindy Peralta." Video editor in Los Angeles who loves turning footage into something that makes people feel something. Drawn to stories about people, culture, identity, and the little moments that make us human. Background in Cinema and Media Arts; post-production with Mainstay Films; interned with Kappa Studios' post team. "Great editing isn't just about what you cut, it's about what you make the audience feel." Likes taking an idea from a timeline full of footage to something cohesive, emotional and memorable.
- Tools: Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, Avid Media Encoder, DaVinci Resolve.
- Location: Los Angeles, CA.
